Data Story 1: TV Energy Consumption Insights

Exploring how screen size, brand efficiency, and display technology impact energy usage across Australian television models.

Target Audience & Story Context

This data story is designed for consumers seeking energy-efficient appliances, policymakers and regulators tracking energy efficiency trends, and researchers studying consumer electronics power consumption.

By analyzing registered specifications from the Australian Government Energy Rating database, this story uncovers how television power draw scales in the real world and provides actionable guidance for reducing household electricity footprints.

1. The Screen Size Trade-Off

As physical screen dimensions increase, power consumption surges. The visual data scale below demonstrates how stepping up through screen size classes dramatically increases annual energy consumption and estimated yearly running costs (calculated at ~$0.25/kWh).

Conclusion & Recommendations

  • Find the Sweet Spot: Sticking to a 55-inch class screen provides an optimal viewing experience while keeping annual power use (~339 kWh) well below larger, power-hungry tiers.
  • Evaluate Beyond Star Ratings: Consumers and policymakers should look at absolute screen size and active wattage alongside star ratings to fully understand real-world energy impacts.
  • Make Informed Choices: Always cross-reference official energy rating labels before purchasing to balance entertainment needs with sustainability goals.
