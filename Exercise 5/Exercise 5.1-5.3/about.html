About Us

Helping Australians make smarter energy choices, one appliance at a time.

Our Mission

At PowerWatch, our mission is to empower Australian households with clear, actionable information about appliance energy consumption. We believe that understanding your energy use is the first step toward reducing your bills and your environmental footprint.

Whether you're choosing a new television, comparing fridge efficiency, or just trying to lower your monthly power bill, we're here to help.

Who We Are

We are a small team of energy enthusiasts, data nerds, and web developers passionate about sustainability. This website was built as part of a web development exercise to demonstrate skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and project organisation.

The project is open-source and available on GitHub. We encourage others to learn from it, contribute, and build their own energy-focused tools.

Data Sources & Accuracy

Energy consumption figures and star ratings are based on publicly available data from the Australian Government's Energy Rating website (energyrating.gov.au) and manufacturer specifications. While we strive for accuracy, actual consumption may vary based on usage patterns, settings, and other factors.

All costs are estimated using the average Australian electricity price of approximately $0.25 per kilowatt-hour (as of 2026).
